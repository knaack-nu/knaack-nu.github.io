FAQ

Common pharmacy questions.

Can I transfer a prescription to Knaack Nu?+

The site includes a transfer-request workflow for eligible prescriptions. The pharmacy must verify transferability, remaining refills and any legal restrictions.

Can the website tell me which medicine I should take?+

No. The catalog is informational and not personalized medical advice. Ask a pharmacist or clinician about suitability for you.

Are the catalog prices final?+

No. Prices shown are demonstration values until verified against real inventory and pricing.

What if I have a medical emergency?+

Do not use this site for emergency care. Contact emergency services or an appropriate urgent medical resource.
